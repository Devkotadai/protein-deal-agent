# Price per gram of protein

Deals are always ranked by what you pay for each gram of protein, never by price per ounce or per serving. Weight-based pricing rewards tubs full of fillers, carbs, and flavoring; price per gram of protein rewards what you actually want.

## Formula

```
total protein (g) = grams protein per serving × servings per container × pack count
$/g protein       = final price ÷ total protein (g)
$ per 25 g        = $/g protein × 25
$ per 100 g       = $/g protein × 100
```

- **Final price** is the price after any sale, coupon, or promo code. Add shipping when it isn't free and it changes the ranking.
- **Pack count** is 1 for a single tub; use it for multipacks of bars or shakes.
- Read protein per serving and servings per container from the nutrition label. Never estimate protein from the container weight. If either number can't be confirmed, the deal is dropped.
- **$ per 25 g** is roughly the cost of one typical scoop or shake, which makes deals easy to compare at a glance.

## Example

A 5 lb whey tub, 24 g protein per serving, 74 servings, on sale for $49.99:

```
total protein = 24 × 74 = 1,776 g
$/g           = 49.99 ÷ 1,776 = $0.028
$ per 25 g    = $0.70
$ per 100 g   = $2.81
```

## Display

- $/g to 3 decimals (e.g. `$0.028`)
- $ per 25 g and $ per 100 g to 2 decimals
